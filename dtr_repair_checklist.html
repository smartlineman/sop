DTR Repair Checklist

Pre-Work

  • Confirm PTW and isolation
  • Verify transformer ID / rating
  • Inspect earthing and barricading

Repair Tasks

  • Check HT/LT bushing health
  • Inspect jumpers and terminations
  • Record oil level and leakage observations

Post-Work

  • Tighten all hardware and clean area
  • Remove earth and restore supply in sequence
  • Update maintenance register / app
